# Ringing bell v2 — short, reckless (fresh T2I)

A monster bell in a black void, ringing like it wants to hurt the dark. We're underneath it, front and side at once, worm's-eye, dutch tilt, way too close. It's gigantic, it's swinging straight at us, mouth yawning open, clapper flung wild. Pure action. Nothing else exists. No floor, no ground, no horizon, nothing.

The bell is whole. Unbroken skin of iron, smooth and flawless, not one crack, not one fissure, no fractures anywhere, no Liberty Bell damage. The fire isn't in cracks, the fire is in the metal itself: heat blooming under the skin, whole territories of the flank gone molten and napalm-bright, other territories still cold black iron, the glow savage and uneven, migrating like weather. The inside of the mouth is a furnace gone feral. Embers rip off the swing and streak past the lens. This thing has magnitude. It does not care about us at all.

And the sound is visible. Heat waves blast off it and every wave wears the bell's own shape: crown, shoulders, that huge flare of skirt, copied bigger and bigger in warped air, silhouette after silhouette stampeding outward, uneven, lopsided, tearing themselves apart into plasma and smoke, bending the black like spacetime is losing. Power, heat, magnitude, nothing else.
