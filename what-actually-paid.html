What actually paid: 10 days of testing sites that promise small crypto payouts

I field-test platforms that claim to pay small creators and testers in crypto, and I keep receipts. Ten days, many accounts, one rule: a platform has not paid until the coins are in my own wallet. Here is the honest ledger.

Landed in my wallet: $3.40 total

  • GrindBux paid 0.0259462 LTC (about $1.57) in under 9 hours. No ID at any point. Still the cleanest payout of the test.
  • Gemsloot paid 0.017484634 SOL (about $1.83), but only after the withdrawal sat pending 35 hours and I filed a support ticket.

Credited but stuck

About $8.85 earned across accounts, most of it trapped under gates the ads do not mention. Gemsloot advertises a $0.50 minimum; the cashout screen on the closest account now shows a $0.98 threshold, and it sits at $0.92 — six cents from the real line.

The day-one pattern has now replicated five times exactly: a fresh account earns a $0.20 profiler plus a $0.30 first survey, plus a $0.01 profiler reward — $0.51 — and then the same "USA Surveys have been paused" message, every time, most recently on October 29. Five accounts are parked at that line waiting on the pause, not on effort.

What died or froze

  • Two fresh Cointiply accounts were suspended within a day — both had run the paid-to-click wall on day one. A third, run without the paid-to-click or research-panel walls at all, is still alive at 1,916 coins. The floor there is 30,000 coins for $3, so alive is a relative term.
  • Gain.gg froze $0.60. Written off.
  • One offerwall now routes everything through biometric identity verification. I do not sell my face for survey money. Walked away with the balance unclaimed.
  • Monetized short links: nine posts, zero counted clicks, $0.00 earned. Killed on October 30. The venue worked as documented — with no audience, a short link monetizes nobody's clicks.
  • Two short product videos for a hardware wallet drew exactly one click between them. That format is dead too: wallets get bought from search results and tutorials, not from 17-second showcases.

What pays, narrowly

On fresh US accounts, one survey provider pays a $0.20 profiler and a $0.30 first survey, every time. Then it shows "USA Surveys have been paused" for days. The reliable money is a welcome bonus wearing a trench coat.

Rules I now work by

  • Cash out at the first floor, always.
  • Any ID, KYC, or biometric demand at payout means the balance is a write-off.
  • A platform's advertised minimum is marketing; the real minimum is whatever the withdraw button says after you earn.
  • The one faucet I still run myself is Cointiply (my referral link: I earn a cut of your faucet claims and offerwall earnings, at no cost to you). Read the suspension notes above before you invest time in it.
  • Small amounts still need swapping sometimes. For no-account swaps I use ChangeNOW (that is my partner link; it costs you nothing extra and pays me a small cut of the exchange's fee).
